Education

Meera's story

The challenge: Meera, a single mother of two, struggled to afford school supplies and tutoring after a job loss.

SprouHub's contribution: Enrolled both children in our education program for supplies, tutoring, and mentorship.

The outcome: Both children are back on track academically, and Meera now volunteers with our outreach team.
