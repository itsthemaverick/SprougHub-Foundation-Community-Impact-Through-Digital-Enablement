## About SprouHub 🌍

SprouHub believes that lasting change doesn't come from grand gestures, but from small, consistent acts repeated by many people, deeply rooted in real communities. It started as a small group of neighbors organizing weekend clean-ups and supply drives. What began as a handful of small acts grew into a structured, community-led organization working across education, food security, wellbeing, and the environment.

The organization's approach is community-led, emphasizing listening first and building together. Every program starts with the community it serves, not with a predetermined solution. SprouHub works alongside local leaders, tracks outcomes honestly, and reports back transparently.

### Our Values 🌱

- **Community-led:** Solutions come from the people they affect.
- **Transparency:** Honest reporting, even when results are mixed.
- **Human first:** Every number represents a real person.
- **Sustainability:** Built to last beyond any single project.

## Key Features ✨

- **Community Programs:** Engages in six core areas: Community Development, Education, Food & Essentials, Wellbeing, Environment, and Volunteer Programs.
- **Volunteer-Powered:** Relies on a strong network of volunteers contributing time and skills.
- **Transparent Giving:** Provides clear reporting on where donations go and their impact.
- **Impact Dashboard:** Offers a detailed view of key metrics, funds deployed, and active campaigns.
- **Real Stories:** Showcases personal narratives of individuals impacted by SprouHub's work.
- **Digital Enablement:** Leverages digital platforms for communication, engagement, and program delivery.

## Usage ▶️

This website serves as the frontend for the SprouHub Foundation, showcasing its mission, programs, impact, and ways to get involved. Users can:

-   **Learn about SprouHub:** Explore the `about.html` page for the organization's story, values, and team.
-   **Discover Programs:** View the different community programs offered on the `our-work.html` page.
-   **See Impact:** Check the `impact.html` page for a dashboard of key metrics and campaign progress.
-   **Read Stories:** Discover personal stories of impact on the `stories.html` page.
-   **Volunteer:** Learn about volunteer opportunities and sign up via the `volunteers.html` page.
-   **Donate:** Make a donation through the `donate.html` page, with a success confirmation on `donate-success.html`.

### How to use 🚀

The website is designed to be user-friendly and informative. Key sections and their intended use include:

-   **Homepage (`index.html`):** Provides an overview of SprouHub's mission, highlights key impact statistics, and directs users to important sections like donation and impact.
-   **Donation Flow (`donate.html`, `donate-success.html`):** Allows users to make one-time or monthly donations, select amounts, and see the impact of their contribution. The `donate-success.html` page confirms the donation and suggests further engagement.
-   **Volunteer Engagement (`volunteers.html`):** Features a directory of volunteers, highlights current volunteers, and provides a sign-up form for new volunteers. It also showcases various skills that are valuable to the organization.
-   **Impact Reporting (`impact.html`):** Displays key performance indicators, fund allocation by program, and active campaign progress, emphasizing transparency.
